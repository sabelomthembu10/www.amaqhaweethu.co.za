Who we are

About AmaQhawe ETHU

We exist to create stronger, safer and more caring communities for a better tomorrow.

AmaQhawe ETHU Non Profit Organization is committed to uplifting, protecting and serving children, youth and families. Through practical support, partnerships and community-centred programmes, we help people access dignity, opportunity, skills and hope.
